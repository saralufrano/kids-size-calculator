Privacy Policy

What Size Will They Be? is a simple clothing and shoe size calculator designed to work without collecting personal information.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the app.

How the app works

Calculations are performed on your device using the size or age information you enter. That information is not sent to us or stored on a server.

Accounts, analytics, ads, and tracking

  • No account or login is required.
  • No advertising is included.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking tools are included.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or other personal device data.

Children's privacy

The app may be used by parents or caregivers to estimate children's clothing and shoe sizes, but it does not collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in the future, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be submitted through the project's public GitHub repository.
